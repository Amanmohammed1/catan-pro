# M4 — Polish

**Goal (PLAN.md):** the part that separates a demo from a game people ask to
play again. Animations driven by the server's event stream, sound, a trade panel
that works the way colonist.io's does, a log that points at the board, rematch,
spectators.

Taken after M5, so the six-seat layout was already in place.

## What was built

### Animation, off the event stream

`game/useEventCues.ts` turns the log into short-lived cues, and everything that
moves hangs off them (CLAUDE.md golden rule 6: the event log is the animation
trigger). A player who just reconnected, a spectator and the player who acted
all see the same thing, and the client cannot animate something the server did
not say happened.

- Hexes that just paid out flash on the board after a roll.
- Resource cards that just arrived lift, and show what they gained.
- A reconnect replaces the whole log at once; a batch bigger than one action's
  worth is treated as history and passes silently, rather than replaying a whole
  game's animation in a single frame.

The dice already landed on the server's value (M3); nothing about that changed.

### Sound

Nine CC0 clips from Kenney — casino audio, interface sounds, music jingles —
vendored in `packages/assets/audio` with `LICENSE.txt` naming every file's
source, and imported by URL so Vite bundles them. Nothing is fetched from
anyone else's server at runtime.

Triggered from the same cues, never from a click handler. A clip that will not
load degrades to silence. Browsers refuse to start audio before a gesture, so
the first click unlocks it and anything earlier is dropped rather than queued.
Sound and volume are saved settings, with a toggle in the bar.

### Trading

Click a card to put it on the table, click it again to take it back — no
steppers, no typing — with the bank's best rate shown beside the offer, because
that is what every player is silently comparing against.

The rules allow haggling (p.4), so the engine does too: `counterTrade` lets a
responder answer with terms of their own, and the offering player can close on a
counter exactly as on an acceptance. Validated rather than enumerated, like
`offerTrade` (ADR 0003). 11 tests.

No new server timer was needed: `autoPassAction` already declines or withdraws a
trade nobody answers, on the tick the turn timer runs on.

### The log points at the board

Hovering — or focusing, so it is not mouse-only — a log line lights up the road,
intersection or hexes it is about. `game/eventTargets.ts` is a pure mapping from
event to board ids; lines that are not about a place highlight nothing rather
than guessing.

### Rematch

Same people, same seats, new board. Host only, and only once the game is
decided — a rematch mid-game would let a losing host wipe the board. The new
match gets its own id so the finished game's command log stays intact and
replayable, and a new seed with a fresh commitment, because the old seed has
already been revealed. Hot-seat gets the same button, backed by a local reset.

## Not done

- **Spectator mode.** `WireView.you` is a seat id the whole client reads;
  making it nullable touches every panel. Deferred rather than half-built — see
  the handover.
- **Frame rate on a low-powered machine** still has not been measured. The
  meter is in the bar and `PerformanceMonitor` drops effects automatically.
- Cards do not physically fly between player cards on a steal; the theft is
  announced, sounded and logged instead.
